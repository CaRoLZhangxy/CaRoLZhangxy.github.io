About

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Weile Jia. I received my undergraduate degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences with a major in Computer Science and Technology.

My research interests include AI for Science (AI4S), High-Performance Computing, and Machine Learning Systems. In AI4S, I am particularly interested in designing, implementing, and optimizing training and inference systems for Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials. I also work on performance optimization of First-Principles Calculations and Molecular Dynamics applications on modern supercomputers.

I am currently working on distributed LLM pretraining systems on heterogeneous computing platforms. Please feel free to contact me.
